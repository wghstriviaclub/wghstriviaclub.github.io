Curiosity brings us together.

The Walnut Grove Trivia Club is a fun, welcoming place to learn unexpected facts, work as a team, and compete without academic pressure.

?
?✦✦

Built from a shared love of trivia.

Founder and president Durvish Yadagiri started the club with club sponsor and advisor Koob Yohannes (“Mr. Yo”), an AP World History teacher. Their shared interest in fun trivia games became a club that brings many subjects—and many kinds of thinkers—together.

“There is absolutely no academic stress or pressure in the club.”

Our mission

We provide a welcoming environment where students of every skill level can test their knowledge, learn new facts, make friends, and collaborate in friendly trivia competitions.

What makes us different

Questions span pop culture, history, science, sports, and more. Every game makes room for different strengths, and the goal is always fun—not grades or pressure.
